PROFILE / 2026

I am a recent architecture graduate with a strong interest in designing spaces that are both thoughtful and emotionally resonant. My approach to architecture is grounded in careful observation of context, climate, materiality, and the ways people experience and inhabit space.

Through academic and design explorations, I have built a process that values clarity, spatial sensitivity, and purposeful simplicity. I am particularly interested in projects that engage with landscape, healing environments, adaptive experiences, and human-centered planning strategies where architecture supports both function and atmosphere.

I see architecture as an evolving practice of questioning, learning, and refinement. This portfolio reflects my exploration of design as a meaningful and responsible discipline balancing conceptual thinking, technical understanding, and practical decision-making.
